THE SPIRIT OF CHRISTMAS

Christmas brings us together.

Christmas is a celebration of love, hope, peace and togetherness.

The Manaveeyam Christmas Cultural Collective 2026 brings the festive spirit alive at Manaveeyam Veedhi, Thiruvananthapuram.

Join us for four evenings of cultural performances, music, dance, drama and artistic expressions celebrating the joy of Christmas.

Come together with friends, family and the community to celebrate the season!

FOUR EVENINGS OF CELEBRATION

Experience the Festive Spirit

Music, movement, creativity and performances come together to celebrate Christmas at Manaveeyam.

Christmas Carols

Celebrate the season with the joy and harmony of Christmas songs.

Dance

Colourful performances and energetic festive expressions.

Skits & Drama

Stories and creative performances celebrating the spirit of Christmas.

Live Band

Music and live performances to make the evenings memorable.

And many more creative expressions of Christmas!
